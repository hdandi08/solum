Rules — Never Break

  • ✕ Never lowercase the wordmark — SOLUM is always uppercase.
  • ✕ Never recolour the wordmark — flat white or flat black only. No gradient, glow, outline or monogram.
  • ✕ Never re-type the wordmark in a font — always use the supplied logo file.
  • ✕ Never use orange, amber, yellow or green. The accent is always blue.
  • ✕ Never use the word "soap" anywhere.
  • ✕ Never use Arabic script.
  • ✕ No figure or person on product boxes.
  • ✓ Keep clear space around the wordmark — at least the height of the "S" on all sides.
  • ✓ White wordmark on dark, black wordmark on light. Always full contrast.
