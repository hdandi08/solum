Your Business & Objectives
Describe your business, outlining the different product(s) and/or service(s) you offer

SOLUM is a men's premium body care brand selling structured shower ritual kits direct-to-consumer at bysolum.co.uk. The business is currently operating as a sole trader (limited company registration pending with Companies House). I own 100% of the business. The business has not yet started trading — the Start Up Loan will fund the first inventory order and launch marketing.

The product lineup consists of 8 SKUs organised into a two-ritual system: a 10-minute Daily Ritual and a 22-minute Weekly Deep Ritual. Products include a sulphate-free amino acid body wash (manufactured in UK by Cosmiko), exfoliating mitt and back scrub cloth (Korean bathhouse tradition, manufactured in China), a silicone scalp massager (South Korea, supplier COOLFIN A), rhassoul clay body mask and organic argan oil (both Morocco-sourced), a fast-absorbing body lotion (UK, Cosmiko), and an artisan hand-woven Kese mitt (Turkey, Jennifer's Hamam).

Kits are sold in three tiers:

  • GROUND Kit (Products 01, 02, 03, 04, 07): First box £65, monthly refill subscription £38/mo
  • RITUAL Kit (Products 01–07): First box £85, monthly refill subscription £48/mo
  • SOVEREIGN Kit (All 10 products): First box £130, monthly refill £58/mo — Coming Soon

The business model is: customer buys a first kit (includes tools lasting 6–12 months), then subscribes for monthly consumable refills (body wash, clay mask, argan oil, lotion). Customer acquisition cost is £28, lifetime gross profit at 14 months is £428, giving a 15.3× LTV:CAC ratio. Monthly churn is modelled at 7%.

The business is home-based. No commercial premises are required. Dispatch via Royal Mail Tracked 48 (£3.85/parcel).

Key milestones already achieved: Brand identity locked; website built and live at bysolum.co.uk; full product lineup confirmed; supplier briefs sent to Cosmiko UK (body wash + lotion), COOLFIN A Korea (scalp massager), and Moroccan suppliers (clay + argan oil); logo files, label designs, and ritual card completed; CPSR compliance plan confirmed with assessor shortlist. 180 people have already joined the pre-launch waitlist at bysolum.co.uk, demonstrating validated demand before a single unit has been sold.

Short-term objectives (current year — 2026)
  1. Complete first inventory order of 250 kits and receive stock by July 2026. Total outlay: £6,930 (COGS £6,130 + packaging samples £800).
  2. Launch bysolum.co.uk to paying customers in June/July 2026 — first sale target by 30 June 2026.
  3. Acquire 50 active subscribers by 30 September 2026, generating £1,900/month in recurring revenue.
  4. Complete CPSR compliance reports for all 4 cosmetic products (01, 05, 06, 07) and submit SCPN notification via UK Submit system by 31 July 2026.
Medium-term objectives (next 1–2 years — 2027–2028)
  1. Reach 437 active subscribers by May 2027 (12 months of trading), generating £18,791/month recurring revenue — in line with conservative Year 1 projections.
  2. Scale total revenue to £324,000+ in Year 2 (2027) across kit sales and subscription refills, based on a 60% annual growth rate on new customer acquisition.
  3. Launch SOVEREIGN tier (Products 09 + 10: Turkish Kese mitt and Black Soap) by Q2 2027.
  4. Reduce effective CAC to under £25 by Q2 2027 through growing organic Instagram content driving 35%+ of new customer acquisition without paid spend.
Your Start Up Loan — Describe how you will use it and how it will help achieve objectives

The Start Up Loan will be deployed entirely into product inventory. The founder has already personally invested over £22,000 into the business — covering website development, brand identity, design, compliance, photography, packaging samples, and operational setup. Marketing and ongoing operating costs will continue to be funded from the founder's personal income and growing business revenue. The loan's sole purpose is to unlock a large-MOQ stock order from Cosmiko UK, securing significantly better unit pricing on the two highest-volume consumable products (body wash and lotion), and to fund complementary inventory from all other suppliers to fulfil first kits and subscriptions at scale.

Why MOQ matters: Cosmiko UK pricing improves materially at higher order quantities. A larger order of body wash and lotion reduces per-unit cost, directly improving gross margin on every future sale and every subscription refill. The loan enables an order size that would otherwise require 12+ months of trading to justify — compressing that advantage to Day 1.

Your Target Customers
Briefly describe your target customers (demographic details)

Primary target: Men aged 25–45, UK-based, household income £35,000+. Likely engaged in fitness, gym, or sport. Already spending money on some grooming products (shower gel, deodorant) but have no structured body care system. Comfortable purchasing online — likely already subscribing to other DTC brands (meal kits, coffee, supplements).

Secondary target: Men who already spend on grooming (e.g. Bulldog, Patricks, Hims) but want a more complete, system-based approach. Also: gift-buyers (partners, parents) purchasing a considered gift for men in the 25–45 range.

Geographic focus: UK-wide, primarily England. No physical retail presence in Year 1 — all sales via bysolum.co.uk.

What customer need or problem does your product address?

Most men shower every day and still have rough skin, a neglected back, and a scalp they've never once properly cleaned. This isn't laziness — no brand has ever built them a system worth following. Every men's grooming brand focuses on face, hair, or fragrance. Nobody has built a complete, sequenced body care system for men.

SOLUM solves this by delivering a specific daily and weekly ritual in a single box: the right tools, the right formulations, and step-by-step instructions. The customer doesn't need to research what to buy or in what order to use it — the system is complete on arrival.

Explain your approach to pricing

SOLUM is positioned at premium-accessible — above mass-market (Bulldog, Simple) but below luxury (Aesop, Noble Isle). Pricing is justified by ingredient sourcing (Moroccan organic argan oil, Korean bathhouse techniques, UK amino acid formulations) and the system value — customers are not buying individual products, they are buying a complete ritual.

  • GROUND Kit first box: £65 — gross margin 69%. Tools (exfoliating mitt, back cloth, scalp massager) are included and last 6–12 months.
  • RITUAL Kit first box: £85 — gross margin 70%. Adds weekly-ritual products (clay mask, argan oil).
  • Monthly refills: £38–£48 — consumables only (body wash, lotion, clay mask, argan oil). Gross margin 48–64%.

Benchmarked: Bulldog starter kits £15–20 (mass market, face-focused). Patricks men's products £25–55/item (premium, hair-focused, no system). SOLUM is the only brand offering a complete body ritual kit at a single accessible price point.

Notes / Commentary for CFF Tab

Cash flow starts June 2026. The £25,000 loan is drawn down in full at the starting point and immediately deployed into stock: £22,000 in M1 (primary Cosmiko body wash + lotion high-MOQ order, Moroccan clay and argan oil, packaging) and £3,000 in M2 (Korean tools and Turkish Kese mitt). This front-loads the stock investment to unlock the best MOQ pricing from day one.

The founder personally funds all marketing, photography, insurance, compliance, and other operating costs — these do not flow through the business cash account. A £5,000 personal contribution in M2 bridges the early-stage period while subscription revenue builds. From M3 onwards, business revenue covers all remaining outflows without further founder injection.

Revenue grows from £1,825 at launch to £21,390 by Month 12 as the subscriber base compounds. The business cash position is always positive, reaching £64,219 at the end of Month 12.

No salary is drawn from the business — the founder's employment income covers all personal expenses. Loan repayments of £2,152/month (12-month term at 6% p.a.) are fully serviceable from the founder's salary with no dependence on business revenue. The 12-month term was chosen to minimise total interest (£824 total vs ~£4,000+ on a 5-year term).
