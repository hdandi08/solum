## The outcome arc (the whole strategy in one line)

**I feel it → I can see it → others smell it → others say it.**

Every video lands on ONE outcome from this ladder. Later outcomes are stronger
than earlier ones because someone else confirms them. A friend noticing beats
any claim we could make.

| Level | Outcome | Example line (creator's own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skin feels better | "My skin stopped feeling tight by day 4" |
| 2 | The back is actually clean | "First time in my life my back is not a problem area" |
| 3 | Smell lasts | "4pm and I still smell like I just showered" |
| 4 | People notice | "My girlfriend asked what I changed. That's the video." |

## Three video concepts (one outcome each, don't stack all four in one video)

### 1. "The back" (Level 2 — most visual, most relatable)
- Hook (0–2s): "Nobody talks about how you're supposed to clean your back."
- Middle: the back cloth in the shower, both handles, the saw motion. Real steam, real bathroom.
- Landing: "Two weeks. The bacne spots are going. This is the only thing I changed."

### 2. "The 4pm test" (Level 3 — the smell claim, time-anchored)
- Hook (0–2s): "It's 4pm. I showered at 7."
- Middle: quick cut of the morning ritual (mitt + wash, 3–4 seconds each step), then back to now.
- Landing: "Dead skin is what odour bacteria feed on. Remove it and the smell has nothing to live on."

### 3. "Someone noticed" (Level 4 — the strongest, save for creators who genuinely have this story)
- Hook (0–2s): "My mate asked me what cologne I'm wearing. I'm not wearing any."
- Middle: what the ritual is, fast. Numbered products, the order, ten minutes.
- Landing: "It's not fragrance. It's just actually being clean for the first time."

## Structure rules (every video)
- **Hook is the outcome, first 2 seconds.** Never open with the brand, the box, or "I got sent this".
- Show the ritual in a real shower. Steam, water, real bathroom. Not a shelf flatlay.
- Say the honest timeline: outcomes at week 1–2, not overnight.
- One outcome per video. The series covers the ladder, not each video.
- End on the outcome restated, not on a discount or a hard sell.

## Claims you can make (true, specific)
- Dead skin builds up and odour bacteria feed on it. Removing it reduces smell at the source.
- The back cloth reaches what hands never do.
- Week 1: dead skin visibly rolls off with the mitt. Week 2: smell noticeably reduces.
- Lotion within 3 minutes of towelling: skin absorbs far more moisture while warm.

## Don'ts
- No "soap", no "skincare", no "pamper". It's a body ritual.
- No price on screen or in caption (link in bio handles conversion).
- No overnight-transformation claims, no medical claims (nothing about curing acne).
- No comparing men to women ("women have routines..."). It's men, for men.
- No dashes in captions. Max 5 hashtags.

## Video content guidelines — long version (reference)

**Feel:** Film it like you're telling a friend, not selling to a stranger. Phone
camera, natural light or bathroom light, one take energy. If it looks produced,
it's wrong. Talk to the camera the way you'd voice-note a mate.

**Open on the result, not the product (0–2s).** First line is what changed for
you: "It's 4pm and I still smell like I just showered." Never open with the box,
the brand name, an unboxing, or "I got sent this". No greetings, no "hey guys".

**Then earn it (2–20s).** Show the actual ritual in your actual shower: the mitt
with real water, the back cloth motion, the 3 minute lotion window. Real steam,
real bathroom, imperfect is good. Talk over it in your own words. Use our claims
sheet for the why (dead skin feeds odour bacteria) but say it how you'd say it.

**Land the outcome again (last 3–5s).** Restate what changed and when it changed:
"Two weeks in, this is the only thing I did differently." End there. No discount
codes, no "link in bio" speech, no hard sell.

**One outcome per video.** Pick one: skin feels better / back actually clean /
smell lasts all day / someone noticed. Don't stack them.

**Honesty is the strategy.** Real timelines (week 1–2, not overnight). If your
mate or partner genuinely noticed, that's your video. If they didn't, use a
level 1–3 outcome instead. Never script a fake reaction.

**Practical:**
- 9:16 vertical, 21–34 seconds
- Your face and your voice at some point (talking head or voiceover, either works)
- Captions/subtitles on
- No music that drowns the voice, TikTok native sounds are fine
- Keep the shower shots PG: shoulders up or behind frosted glass
