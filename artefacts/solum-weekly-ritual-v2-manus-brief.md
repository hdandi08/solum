# SOLUM — Weekly Ritual (v2) · Manus Video Production Brief

> Updated weekly ritual flow. For feeding to Manus to generate step-by-step
> educational videos. Last updated: 2026-06-26.

**Format:** 4 short educational clips, one per step. Vertical 9:16 and/or 16:9.

**Visual system (must hold across all clips):** SOLUM black ground `#08090B`, bone
type `#F0ECE2`, **gold accent `#C8A96E`** (weekly = gold, never blue), Bebas Neue
wordmark, Barlow Condensed captions. No person's face hero, hands/body only. Warm
bathroom light, matte black bottles, water on skin. Calm, deliberate pacing — this
is the slow ritual, not the daily.

**Global on-screen furniture:** top-left small `SOLUM` wordmark; bottom strip step
counter `STEP 0X / 04` in gold; product chip lower-right showing the product number
badge + name when a product is in use.

**Products used:** `05 Atlas Clay Mask` · `04 Scalp Massager` · `02 Italy Towel Mitt`
· `06 Argan Body Oil` · `01 Body Wash`. (No Kese / back cloth / body lotion in the
weekly v2.)

> ⚠️ **One-fourth container math:** 1/4 jar covers **half the body**, so the clay
> alternates **upper body / lower body** week to week
> (Wk1 upper → Wk2 lower → Wk3 upper → Wk4 lower). This must be made visually
> unmistakable in Clip 3.

## CLIP 1 — "Mix the Clay" (~25–35s)
**Purpose:** Teach the exact clay mix, including the argan oil addition.

**Products on screen:** `05 Atlas Clay Mask` (jar), `06 Argan Body Oil` (dropper
bottle), a mixing bowl, warm water.

**Shot list:**
1. Close top-down: open the **05** jar, scoop **one-quarter of the container** into
   a black bowl. On-screen measure graphic: `¼ JAR`.
2. Pour **warm water** in slowly, a little at a time.
3. Add **4–5 drops of 06 Argan Oil** from the dropper — count the drops on screen
   `4–5 DROPS`.
4. Stir with fingers/spatula. Lift the mix to show it falling slowly off the tool.
5. Hold on the final texture.

**On-screen text:** `¼ jar Atlas Clay` → `+ warm water, slowly` →
`+ 4–5 drops Argan Oil` → `Mix to thick yoghurt — spreadable, holds its shape`

**Voiceover:** "Start with a quarter of the jar. Add warm water slowly, then four
to five drops of argan oil. Mix to a thick yoghurt — spreadable, but it should hold
its shape. Set it aside."

**Accuracy callouts:** ¼ jar (not the whole jar). Argan goes IN the mix here.
Consistency = thick yoghurt / holds shape.

## CLIP 2 — "Cleanse First" (~20–30s)
**Purpose:** Prep skin with the mitt + body wash before clay.

**Products on screen:** `02 Italy Towel Mitt`, `01 Body Wash`.

**Shot list:**
1. Wet the **02** mitt under the shower, slip it onto the hand.
2. Pump **01 Body Wash** onto the mitt.
3. Long, firm circular strokes — arms, chest, stomach, legs. Show the textured mitt
   weave on skin.
4. Quick rinse of suds, leaving skin **damp, not streaming wet**.

**On-screen text:** `Wet the Italy Towel Mitt` → `Add Body Wash` →
`Scrub the body — long, firm strokes` → `Rinse — leave skin damp`

**Voiceover:** "Before the clay, scrub down with the Italy Towel mitt and your body
wash. Long, firm strokes. Rinse off, but leave your skin damp — not soaking."

**Accuracy callout:** End state is **damp, not fully wet** — this sets up Clip 3.

---

## CLIP 3 — "Clay, Scalp & Deep Massage" (~40–55s) — *the hero clip*
**Purpose:** Apply clay to **this week's half of the body**, oil the scalp, and do
the deep scalp massage while the clay sets.

**Products on screen:** clay bowl (from Clip 1), `06 Argan Oil`, `04 Scalp Massager`.

**Shot list:**
1. **Alternation graphic first** — a body silhouette splits into two halves.
   Animated label: `THIS WEEK: UPPER BODY` (with a small 4-week cycle indicator:
   ● upper / ○ lower / ● upper / ○ lower). *This is the swappable element — see note.*
2. Apply clay by hand to the **highlighted half only** (e.g. chest, shoulders,
   arms, upper back) on **damp** skin.
3. Cut to scalp: **3–4 drops of 06 Argan Oil** dropped onto the scalp, `3–4 DROPS`.
4. Pick up **04 Scalp Massager** — deep, firm circular massage, zone by zone.
   On-screen timer ring counts the window: `5–8 MIN`.
5. Cutaway to the clay on the skin: caption `Don't let it fully dry — no cracks`.
6. Step under water, **rinse off fully** — clay and oil gone.

**On-screen text:** `This week: UPPER body only (¼ jar = half the body)` →
`Apply clay to damp skin` → `3–4 drops Argan Oil on scalp` →
`Deep scalp massage · 5–8 min` → `Don't let the clay crack` → `Rinse fully`

**Voiceover:** "Spread the clay over this week's half of your body — upper body this
week, lower body next. While it's on, put three to four drops of argan oil into your
scalp and work it in with the massager. Go deep for five to eight minutes — that's
your clay timer. Don't let it dry out or crack. Then rinse everything off."

**Accuracy callouts:** Clay on **half the body only**, alternating weekly. Argan on
**scalp** here = a different application than the clay mix and the finish. Massage =
**5–8 min**, ends before cracking. Rinse fully before Clip 4.

## CLIP 4 — "Argan Oil Finish" (~20–30s)
**Purpose:** Seal with argan oil on damp skin.

**Products on screen:** `06 Argan Body Oil`.

**Shot list:**
1. Towel **lightly — stay damp**.
2. **3–4 drops** into the palm, `3–4 DROPS AT A TIME`, warm between hands.
3. Press into the legs first — **press and hold, don't rub** — then work up.
4. Add another 3–4 drops as needed for the next area.
5. Final hold on calm, finished skin. End card: gold `SOLUM` wordmark +
   `Weekly ritual complete`.

**On-screen text:** `Towel off lightly — stay damp` → `3–4 drops at a time` →
`Press in, don't rub — legs first, work up` → `No lotion today`

**Voiceover:** "Finish with the argan oil. Three to four drops at a time — legs
first, then work up. Press it in, don't rub. No lotion today; the oil is enough."

**Accuracy callouts:** **3–4 drops at a time** (repeat per area). Press, don't rub.
No body lotion on weekly days.
