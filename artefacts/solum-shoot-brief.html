Tab 07
The Feeling We Are After

Not clean like a hospital. Clean like you have earned it.

The man in this shoot does not smile for the camera. He does not perform. He moves through his routine with the quiet certainty of someone who knows exactly what they are doing. After the ritual, he looks different. Not glowing. Not styled. Just settled. Ready. That is the feeling every video and photo should land on.

Rules That Cannot Be Broken
Never on this shoot
  • No orange, amber, yellow, or green anywhere in frame. Check props, backgrounds, and wardrobe.
  • No bright clinical whites. Dark surfaces only. Off-white towels acceptable.
  • No ring lights. They destroy the mood we are building.
  • No influencer energy. No enthusiasm, no thumbs up, no "guys".
  • No word "soap" in any caption or spoken content.
  • No figure from the neck up for unboxing. Hands only.

Set and Surface

Surface: Dark matte slate or deep charcoal. No bright whites. No wood grain. Nothing that competes with the products.

Hands: Manicured. Neutral. No nail polish. No jewellery. The hands are part of the product experience.

Lighting: One key light from above and slightly to the side. Products should cast a small shadow. The box interior should feel like it glows faintly when the lid is lifted.
