## 5. Production pipeline (ffmpeg — what I render on approval)

All exports: H.264 yuv420p, `-movflags +faststart`. Three crops where relevant: **9:16** (1080×1920), **4:5** (1080×1350), **1:1** (1080×1080).

1. **Stills** → crop bucket frames to 4:5 / 1:1, optional text + wordmark overlay (Bebas Neue, blue/bone on black).
2. **Carousels** → 4:5 slide sets; slide 1 hook, 2–4 info, 5 kit + CTA.
3. **Slideshows** → photo sequence, Ken Burns pan/zoom, caption per slide, music bed, 9:16 (+1:1 alt).
4. **Reels** → product films already 9:16: add captions + wordmark + CTA card. Banner: `crop` 4K→9:16 (or blurred-pad), then cut to 6/9/15/30s.

**Output:** `artefacts/social/<week>/<post-id>_<format>.{mp4,jpg}` + a per-post caption/hashtag `.txt`.
